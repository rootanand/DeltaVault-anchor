DeltaVault DPDP demo, published checkpoints

This is a demonstration. Every key is derived from a fixed one-byte seed, so anybody can reproduce them and nobody should trust them. The checkpoints below are published by the demo's operator from a laptop. This page is the operator's own feed, which annex A1 section 7.2 classifies as operator-local, and it is here so a challenging party can read a commitment root that was committed before they ever challenged it.

Restarted under the post-quantum format. The 21 checkpoints published here before were signed under the old Ed25519-only format and have been removed. Every checkpoint from now on is signed with a classical and a post-quantum signature pair.
